The idea

Stay local. Sell further.

Every member keeps their own brand, owners and city, and closes deals under local law. The network adds what one office cannot: listings from other cities and clients from other markets.

Share listings

Your listings appear on other members' websites. When their client buys or rents, the commission is shared by the role each side played.

Refer clients

A client wants a home in another city? Refer them to the local member, who handles everything there and pays you a referral fee.

  1. RegisterThe referring member logs the client. The timestamp protects their right.
  2. MatchThe client picks a home on the platform, in their language, matched to personality and birth chart.
  3. CloseThe local member shows the home and closes under local law.
  4. ShareIncome is shared as agreed, in one ledger every side can see.

Principles

Local, legal, in writing.

Each member covers their own area Each agency works only where it is entitled or properly licensed to. The central team only builds the platform; it never brokers in competition with members or takes their clients.

Clear roles All viewings and on-the-ground work in Thailand are done by the local member. Partners abroad screen clients and refer them.

Long-term rentals, within the law For property in Thailand the network takes long-term rental contracts only, to stay within the law (no lets under 30 days, which count as hotel business).

Transparent and safe Every partnership is agreed in writing before the first deal, and client data is passed on only with the client's consent.

Same data and price everywhere A co-listed home must carry the owner's real data and the same price on every website, and once it is booked or sold it comes off every site on the same day.
